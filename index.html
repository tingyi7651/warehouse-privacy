Warehouse Racks Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

"Warehouse Racks" (the "App") requires no sign-up, does not upload the rack and stock data you enter, and has no ads. That data stays on your own device. If you buy the Pro version, payment is handled by Apple or Google, and RevenueCat is used to confirm the purchase status. See section 3.

1. What data we collect

We do not collect the data you enter, and we do not ask you for personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

2. Where your data is stored

The racks, specifications, units, color settings and stock quantities you create in the App are stored only in the App's local storage on your device. They are never sent to our servers or to any third party.

3. Pro purchase and third-party services

Everyday use needs no internet connection. The App goes online only when you buy or restore the Pro version, or when it checks your purchase status at launch. Purchases are handled by:

  • Apple App Store and Google Play: process payment. We cannot see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com): helps us verify purchases and record whether you own Pro. During a purchase, RevenueCat may receive a randomly generated anonymous user ID, purchase records (product, time, platform), the App version, device type and the IP address used to connect. We use this data only to confirm purchase status. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

The rack and stock content you enter is never sent to RevenueCat or any other third party.

4. Backup and import

The App offers "export backup file", "copy backup text" and "import backup". A backup contains all the data you entered, and you decide where to keep it (for example phone files, notes or messages). You are responsible for exported files, and we cannot access them. If you send a backup to someone else or store it in a cloud service, that service's provider is responsible for its privacy practices.

5. Data deletion

You can delete racks and stock inside the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently deletes everything stored on the device. Please export a backup before changing phones or clearing data. Your Pro purchase record is kept in your Apple or Google account and can be recovered with "Restore purchase" after reinstalling.

6. Children's privacy

The App does not ask anyone, including children under 13, for personal information, and shows no ads.

7. Changes to this policy

If future changes affect how data is handled, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email: tingyi7651@gmail.com
